1. Overview

Welcome to A·chik Sda Gitrang (Garo SDA Hymnal). This privacy policy governs your use of the application available on the Google Play Store, designed to provide Christian hymns, worship songs, and music for personal and congregational worship.

Our Privacy Commitment: We respect your privacy completely. We do not collect, store on external servers, transmit, sell, or share any personal information or sensitive user data.

2. Information Collection and Storage

a. Personal Identification

There is no account creation, no registration, and no user login required to use the application.

If you choose to enter an optional nickname during onboarding or in settings, this name is stored strictly on your local device via Android Preferences (DataStore). It is never sent to any server or external service.

b. Financial & Payment Information (Google Play Purchases)

When you purchase A·chik Sda Gitrang on the Google Play Store, all transactions and payment processing are handled exclusively and securely by Google LLC through Google Play Billing.

The developer does not collect, see, or store your credit card numbers, debit card details, bank information, or billing address. All payment information is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service.

c. Favorites and Customizations

Any preferences you configure (including your favorite hymns list, custom font sizes, selected themes, and recently viewed hymns) are saved exclusively within your device's local storage database (Room/DataStore).

Uninstalling the application or clearing the application cache will completely remove all of this locally stored data.

3. Audio Streaming & Offline Downloads

The application connects to the Internet solely to stream or download audio files (tunes) of hymns to your device for offline playback.

We do not monitor, log, or track your reading habits, playback history, or hymn searches.

4. Permissions Used in the App

The application requests only minimal, standard Android permissions required to deliver its core worship features:

a INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

Required to check connection status and download or stream audio files and Google Fonts.

b FOREGROUND_SERVICE & MEDIA_PLAYBACK

Allows hymn music to continue playing continuously in the background when your screen is locked or when you switch apps.

c FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC

Used to download hymn audio tracks in the background without getting stopped by OS power management.

d POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+)

Exclusively used to display media playback controls (Play, Pause, Skip) and download progress in your system notification tray.

e WAKE_LOCK

Ensures playback does not abruptly stop when the device enters sleep mode.

Note: We never request sensitive permissions such as Location, Camera, Contacts, SMS, or Microphone.

5. Third-Party Services

The app contains no third-party advertising frameworks (such as AdMob) and no user-tracking analytics.

The application utilizes standard Google Play Services for font rendering (Google Fonts) and Android system compatibility. Google's handling of technical diagnostic information is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Children's Privacy

Our app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. It is safe for families, churches, and audiences of all ages.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
