1. Overview & Privacy Commitment

Welcome to A·chik Sda Gitrang (Garo SDA Hymnal). This application is created to facilitate Christian worship, hymnal study, personal devotions, congregational singing, and meaningful spiritual fellowship for the Garo-speaking Christian community worldwide.

Our Sacred Privacy Commitment: We respect your privacy completely. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or exploit your personal information. We do not track your physical location, reading habits, or search queries. The app contains zero third-party advertisement networks and zero commercial user-tracking trackers.

2. Local Hymnal Data & Preferences

For the core hymnal experience, the app operates entirely on your local device without requiring an account or sign-in:

  • No Mandatory Account Creation: You can read, search, and sing all hymns without providing any email address, phone number, password, or real name.
  • Favorites & Bookmarks: Your marked favorite hymns and personal bookmarks are saved exclusively in your device's private local database (Room / DataStore).
  • Display & Font Preferences: Custom font sizes, dark mode preferences, and audio player settings remain strictly inside your device sandbox and are never transmitted to external servers.
  • Local Reset: Uninstalling the app or clearing application storage in Android system settings permanently wipes all locally stored preferences.

3. Community Songbook Chat & Public Fellowship

The app features a built-in Community Songbook Chat to enable Garo hymn lovers and church members to connect, share hymn reflections, request song tunes, and fellowship together.

👤 Guest Profile Information

To join the chat, you voluntarily choose a display name (e.g., "Guest" or your first name) and may optionally choose a profile avatar. No email, password, or phone verification is needed.

💬 Messages & Reactions

Chat messages, timestamps, and emoji reactions you share are published to the group and stored securely in our cloud database (Supabase) so community members can see them.

⏰ Offline Message Queuing

If you compose or send a message while offline (no network), the message is held safely on your local device with a WhatsApp-style Clock (🕓) icon until connection returns, at which point it transmits automatically.

🗑 Full User Deletion Rights

You can delete your messages at any time using "Delete for everyone" (removes from the cloud database) or "Delete for me" (removes locally from your device).

Photo Avatar Uploads: If you choose to upload an avatar photo, it is selected via Android's secure Photo Picker and stored in an encrypted cloud storage bucket managed by Supabase exclusively for your chat avatar.

4. Financial & Google Play Billing

If you purchase A·chik Sda Gitrang on the Google Play Store, all financial transactions and payment processing are handled exclusively and securely by Google LLC through the Google Play Store Billing platform.

The developer does not collect, process, see, or store your credit card numbers, debit card details, bank information, or billing address. All payment details are governed directly by Google's Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service.

5. Audio Streaming & Offline Downloads

To provide high-quality musical accompaniment for worship, hymn tunes can be streamed or downloaded directly to your phone:

  • Local Audio Sandbox: Downloaded hymn tunes are stored in the app's isolated private storage directory (Context.getExternalFilesDir / filesDir). Other apps on your device cannot access or modify these files.
  • No Behavioral Tracking: We do not log which hymns you listen to, when you listen, or how many times you replay a track.
  • Storage Management: You can delete any individual downloaded hymn audio or all downloaded hymns at any time directly within the Hymn Detail or Settings screen.

6. Android Permissions Used in the App

The application adheres strictly to Google Play's principle of least privilege, requesting only standard, essential Android permissions:

1 INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

Required to stream hymn audio, check online/offline connectivity status for chat synchronization, and fetch Google Fonts.

2 FOREGROUND_SERVICE & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK

Allows hymn music to continue playing seamlessly in the background when you turn off the screen or use other apps during personal or family devotions.

3 FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC

Enables reliable batch downloading of hymn audio files in the background without interruptions from OS battery optimization.

4 POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+)

Used strictly to display media playback controls (Play, Pause, Rewind 10s, Skip 10s) and download progress in your system notification bar.

5 WAKE_LOCK

Ensures continuous audio playback without abrupt cuts or stutters when the phone enters low-power sleep mode.

No Sensitive Permissions: We never request sensitive or intrusive permissions such as precise GPS Location, Contacts, SMS, Microphone, Phone State, or broad external storage access.

7. Third-Party Services & Infrastructure

The application utilizes trusted, industry-standard infrastructure providers to operate securely:

  • Supabase Inc.: Open-source, enterprise-grade backend infrastructure provider used for the Community Chat database (PostgreSQL), real-time messaging, and avatar storage. Supabase complies with strict international data protection standards (GDPR, SOC2 Type II). Learn more at Supabase Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Services: Used for app distribution, Google Play Billing, and system font rendering (Google Fonts). Governed by Google Privacy Policy.
  • Zero Advertising Networks: We do not integrate Google AdMob, Unity Ads, Meta Audience Network, or any other commercial ad network.

8. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

A·chik Sda Gitrang is built for church congregations, families, youth, and children. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. The app contains no mature content, no violence, no tracking, and no commercial advertisements.

9. Data Retention & User Rights

You maintain full control over your information:

  • Edit & Delete Messages: You can edit or permanently delete your sent chat messages directly inside the chat interface using "Delete for everyone".
  • Profile Clearing: You can reset or update your display name and avatar photo anytime in the chat settings.
  • Complete Data Erasure: To delete all local data, clear the app's cache and data via Android Settings > Apps > A·chik Sda Gitrang > Storage > Clear Storage, or simply uninstall the application.
